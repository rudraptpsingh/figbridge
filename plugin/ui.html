  2. Restart Claude Desktop (or your MCP client).
  3. Come back here and flip Live bridge — the dot turns green.
You can use Figbridge without the bridge too: click Export selection or Export app spec and copy the result. Nothing is sent anywhere unless Live bridge is on, and the bridge runs only on your own machine at http://localhost:7331.
